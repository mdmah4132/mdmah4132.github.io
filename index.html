Creative Statement

As a Media Studies student at Hunter College with a minor in Mathematics, I am interested in exploring how creativity, visual communication, and technology come together to tell stories. I believe images and digital media can communicate emotions, ideas, and experiences in ways that words alone sometimes cannot. Through my creative work, I want to experiment with different visual techniques, including photography, illustration, and digital image editing. I am especially interested in how details such as composition, color, lighting, and expression can influence the way an audience understands an image. I want to learn how to make thoughtful creative decisions and use visual elements to communicate ideas clearly and effectively.

My creative interests also include exploring how people interact with technology, social media, and the world around them. I am interested in how ordinary moments can tell meaningful stories when presented from different perspectives. For example, photographs of everyday life can reveal something about people's experiences, relationships, and the society we live in. I would also like to experiment with combining images and text to communicate messages that encourage viewers to think beyond what they see at first. Throughout this course, my goal is to become more confident using digital creative tools, explore different approaches to image-making, and develop my own visual style. I want my work to be visually interesting while also expressing an idea, feeling, or perspective that an audience can connect with.
